BLACKJACK ROYALE

Beat the dealer to 21 — without going over.

  • Place a bet, then the dealer deals two cards each.
  • Hit to take a card, Stand to keep your hand. Dealer stands on all 17s.
  • Blackjack pays 3 to 2. Double doubles your bet for one final card.
  • Miaw Pair side bet (10 chips): a pair on your first two cards pays 5 to 1, a suited pair 10 to 1.
  • Peek (10 chips): reveal the dealer's hole card once per round.
